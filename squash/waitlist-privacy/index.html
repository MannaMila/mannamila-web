Mila Squash Alpha List Privacy Notice

Effective date: 2026-11-06
Last updated: 2026-11-06
Canonical URL: https://squash.mannamila.com/waitlist-privacy/

The short version

The Mila Squash alpha list is optional and separate from the Mila Squash app and from Meta's alpha access request. If you join, MannaMila LLC uses your response to send occasional news about the alpha, builds that change in response to tester feedback, and store availability, and to see which headsets and kinds of players the alpha is reaching. We do not sell the list, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

Launch-list transition

On November 6, 2026, the earlier Mila Squash launch and availability list became the alpha list. Anyone who joined before that date continues to receive the same categories of email they originally asked for: availability and major product news. No new category of email was added.

What we collect

The signup form asks for:

  • Your email address.
  • Which headset you have: Quest 3, Quest 3S, or another or none yet.
  • How much squash you play, if you choose to say.
  • Your confirmation that you are 18 or older.

Google Forms also records the date and time of your response.

We do not ask for your name, your Meta account, free-form comments, or information about children.

How we use the information

We use the response to tell you about Mila Squash alpha progress, builds that change something testers asked for, and store availability for Meta Quest. We do not send more than one email per calendar month. The headset and playing-frequency answers help us judge who the alpha is reaching; they are not used to decide individual access requests, which are handled on Meta's site.

Who processes the information

MannaMila LLC controls the list. Google Forms and Google Sheets process and store responses on our behalf under Google's Privacy Policy ↗ (opens in a new tab). We do not sell, rent, or use list data for advertising.

Retention

We delete your response 24 months after you sign up unless you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it sooner.

If you use the unsubscribe link in an email, messages stop immediately. If you email us instead, we process the request within five business days. Either way we remove response data that is no longer needed within 30 days and may keep the minimum information needed to honor the opt-out.

Your choices

Every email includes an unsubscribe link. You can also email privacy@mannamila.com to unsubscribe, request a copy of your response, correct it, or ask us to delete it.

The app and the alpha request are separate

Joining the list does not create a Mila Squash account, does not request alpha access, and does not connect your email address to training activity, analytics, diagnostics, or purchases in the app. Alpha access is requested and approved on Meta's site under Meta's terms. For the app's practices, read the separate Mila Squash App Privacy Policy, which also describes this website's analytics.
