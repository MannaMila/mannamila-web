Ball

Flight, contact and court are modeled from published sports-ball measurements and tuned on a Quest 3, on a court built to WSF dimensions. Spin comes off the racket from face angle and the brush of the swing, stays on the ball through bounces and bends the flight through Magnus lift. Drag slows the ball in the air. The ball warms as you hit it and comes off the walls livelier twenty serves in, like a double-yellow.

Racket

Your controller acts as a racket with a face, frame and sweet spot. Contact depends on face angle, swing speed, the racket's effective mass and where the ball hits the strings. Physics steps at 207 Hz with the racket's path swept between steps, and the headset renders at 200 frames per second on Quest 3. A hard-hit ball crosses several times its own diameter between frames, so the timing matters.

Sound

The strings, front wall, side walls, back wall, floor, ceiling and tin each have their own impact sound. The sounds are generated for the game rather than taken from a sample pack. Clean hits and frame hits sound different. The ball's note shifts slightly as it warms. Contact triggers a short vibration in the controller.

Records

Each serve gets a legal or fault verdict and coaching on depth, tightness and tempo. The shot card adds the trajectory, where the ball met the strings and the serve's numbers. Over a session, Stats compares your left and right service boxes, keeps a log you can step through serve by serve, and charts consistency, target accuracy, contact and legality.

Privacy

Your play stays on the headset.

There is no account, no server and no cloud copy of your play. Every serve is recorded on the headset, in encrypted storage, and never uploaded. Nothing about your play is sent to us. Review every serve in Stats.

  • Per serveVerdict, depth, tightness, tempo, ball speed, trajectory, contact point on the strings.
  • Per sessionServe log you can step through, left and right box comparison, personal bests.
  • Over timeConsistency, target accuracy, contact and legality trends. You can delete these records at any time.

Optional crash reports and rough usage counts are the only things that can leave the device, and you can switch them off in Options → Data & Privacy. Full privacy policy.
