Built calm on purpose

No streaks. No badges. No confetti.

Every activity keeps the same quiet pace. Wrong answers are silent — the child simply tries again. There are no timers counting down, no bright flashing rewards, and no sounds a child did not ask for.

Mila Inspire is made for children ages 6 and up. It is an educational app, not a medical or therapeutic product — use it with a parent or caregiver nearby.

For parents

Parents stay in control.

A 4-digit PIN protects all settings. The child-facing side of the app is play only.

  • Parent Stats. See per-activity progress — sessions, first-try accuracy, and how practice is going over time.
  • Hide any tile. Show only the activities that fit your child right now.
  • Family photos. Add your own photos for puzzles and picture cards, from the camera or the photo library.
  • Adjust difficulty. Each activity has its own setup, from gentle starts to bigger challenges.
  • Delete everything. A delete-all option is always available in the parent area.
