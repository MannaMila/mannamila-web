Mila Inspire — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 17, 2026

Who we are

Mila Inspire is made by MannaMila LLC. It is a calm learning app designed for children ages 6 and up, with autism-friendly pacing, for Android tablets (Google Play) and iPad (App Store). The app includes 16 learning activities, plus 4 tiles shown as "coming soon."

Mila Inspire is a one-time purchase ($4.99 in the United States at launch) made through Google Play or the App Store. The store processes that payment; we never see your payment details.

For privacy questions, write to privacy@mannamila.com. For product help, write to support@mannamila.com.

The short version

  • We collect no personal information — from children or from anyone else.
  • The app sends nothing off your device. It works fully offline.
  • No accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads, and no in-app purchases in this version.
  • Child profiles, progress stats, photos, and settings stay on your tablet, behind a parent PIN.
  • You can delete everything from the parent Control Center, or by uninstalling the app.

Children's privacy

Mila Inspire is made for children, so we hold it to the strictest standard we know: we collect no personal information from anyone, including children, and the app transmits nothing.

There is nothing for us to receive. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no feature that sends data over the network. A child using Mila Inspire cannot type a message to anyone, share anything, browse to the open web, or make a purchase.

Because we collect nothing, we also disclose nothing. No data is shared with, sold to, or seen by any third party — there is nothing to share. This is our approach to COPPA (the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and to the Google Play Families and Apple kids' guidelines: rather than handle children's data carefully, we designed the app so children's data never reaches us at all.

Everything a parent can configure — settings, camera features, photos, progress stats — sits behind a 4-digit parent PIN. The child-facing side of the app is play only.

What stays on your device

The app does store a small amount of data — locally, on your tablet, in the app's private storage. None of it leaves the device. Here is each category and why it exists:

Child profiles. A first name or nickname, entered by a parent, so the app can keep each child's progress separate. No last name, no birthdate, no contact details.

Progress stats. Each activity records simple session results (which activity, when, and how it went) in a local database on the tablet. These power the parent-only stats screens so you can see how practice is going. They are numbers on your device, not a profile on a server.

Photos you add. Parents can add photos — from the camera or the photo library — to build custom puzzles, picture cards, and the Family Photos collection. Adding photos is a parent-only feature behind the PIN. Photos are stored on the device and never uploaded anywhere.

Settings. Your preferences (sound, motion, which activities are visible, and so on) and the parent PIN itself.

What we do NOT do

  • No accounts. There is nothing to sign up for and no password to create — only the local parent PIN.
  • No analytics or crash reporting. The app collects no analytics and sends no crash reports. No third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK ships in the app, release builds carry no analytics configuration at all, and an automated check on every release verifies the shipped app contains no active analytics setup and no unexpected service endpoints. If a future version ever offers optional, parent-controlled diagnostics, it will be off by default, disclosed here first, and this policy will be updated before that version reaches any store.
  • No ads. No ad networks, no sponsored content, nothing.
  • No tracking. No advertising identifier, no fingerprinting, no cross-app tracking.
  • No selling data. We hold no data to sell, and we would not sell it if we did.
  • No cloud AI. In this version, no feature sends anything to an AI service. Spoken words come from your tablet's built-in text-to-speech voice, generated on the device.
  • No purchases in this version. This version of the app has no in-app purchases. A future version may offer an optional subscription for additional language features. If it does, it will be shown only to parents, behind the parent PIN gate, and we will update this policy before that version ships.

Permissions we request and why

Photo library So a parent can pick an existing photo for those same features — and, on iPad, save an in-app photo back to the library if the parent chooses. Parents only, behind the PIN

We do not request: microphone, location, contacts, calendar, SMS, or any advertising identifier. On iPad, the app declares camera and photo-library access only — there is no microphone entry at all.

About the "internet" permission. The app works fully offline, and no feature transmits your data. The network permission appears in the app's technical manifest because underlying platform components (such as the system web view and the email links used to contact us) require it to be declared. An automated check at release time verifies the app requests only its small, fixed set of permissions.

Backups and device transfer

Android. App data is excluded from Google backup and from device-to-device transfer entirely — backups are disabled at the system level, with explicit exclusion rules. Your child's data never reaches your Google account.

iPad. Apple may include app data in your family's own encrypted device backup — iCloud or a computer backup — according to your own Apple settings. That backup belongs to you, restores only to your own devices, and is never sent to or seen by us.

Your choices

See and manage everything. Tap the gear on the launcher, enter your parent PIN, and open Manage. From there you can manage items individually — photos, custom puzzles, stats, profiles — or use Delete all child data to wipe everything the app has stored in one step.

Uninstall.

  • On Android, uninstalling deletes everything. There is no backup copy anywhere.
  • On iPad, uninstalling deletes the copy on the device. If your own iCloud or computer backup included the app's data, restoring that backup can bring the data back — that backup is yours and under your control.

Ask us anything. We hold no data about you or your child, so there is nothing for us to look up, correct, or delete on a server. If you have a privacy question, or want to check any claim in this policy, write to privacy@mannamila.com and a person will answer.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes what the app does with data — for example, when the optional language subscription described above arrives — we will update this policy before that version reaches either store, change the "Last updated" date at the top, and describe the change in plain language. The app will never quietly start collecting data in an update.
