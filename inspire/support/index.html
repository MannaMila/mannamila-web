Mila Inspire Support

Mila Inspire is a calm learning launcher for young children — sixteen quiet activities on one screen, designed with autistic children in mind and welcoming to every young learner. It runs on Android tablets and iPad, with every setting behind a parent PIN.

Product help: email support@mannamila.com and a person will answer.

Privacy questions: email privacy@mannamila.com, or read the privacy policy.

Good to know

The app is fully offline — it needs no account, no sign-in, and no internet connection to play, so there is no server-side data for us to look up. When you write in, please tell us your tablet model and what you saw on screen.

The parent area is protected by a 4-digit parent PIN. The default PIN is documented inside the app, and a parent can change it in the parent settings. If the PIN was changed and then forgotten, reinstalling the app resets it — note that reinstalling deletes the app's on-device data, as described in the privacy policy.
